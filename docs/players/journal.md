# Journal

Create Sky 0.1.6 includes Questlog. Press grave accent (`) or select the
journal button above the inventory. Change the key in Controls under Miscellaneous,
Open journal. No book item is required.

Entries are grouped into Materials, Local travel, Processing, Components, Flight
equipment, Fuel systems, Cargo and Electricity. All entries are available when
joining; chapter order is a suggested browsing order. Search locates an entry.
Completed entries remain visible and can be hidden using the journal control.

Inventory conditions check what you carry every half second. Put the stated
quantity in your inventory together. Existing items count; items in chests or
placed machines do not. Completion remains recorded after using the items.
The two interaction entries require a right-click on the named block after the
journal is installed. They do not require completing a delivery or designing a circuit.

Entries do not consume items, grant rewards, unlock recipes, or open popups.
Progress is individual and saved by Questlog. The glider recipe and repair
materials are unchanged: obtain phantom membranes through normal play.

Objective rows use short labels. Read the main description for full item names,
counts and conditions. Inventory counts must be held at once; placed machines
and stored items do not count. The description wraps and scrolls. Compact mode
does not make objective rows wrap.
