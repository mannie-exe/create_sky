# Journal

Press grave accent (`) to open Questlog, or select the
journal button above the inventory. Change the key in Controls under Miscellaneous,
Open journal. No book item is required.

Entries cover Field supplies, Local travel, Workshop, Component production,
Airframes and lift, Propulsion and control, Cargo and handling, Electrical systems
and Other projects. All entries are available when
joining; chapter order is a suggested browsing order. Search locates an entry.
Completed entries remain visible and can be hidden using the journal control.

Inventory conditions check what you carry every half second. Put the stated
quantity in your inventory together. Existing items count; items in chests or
placed machines do not. Completion remains recorded after using the items.
Interaction entries require a right-click on the named block. They do not require
completing a delivery or designing a circuit. Advancement entries accept the named
advancement, including one completed before opening the journal. Read their
conditions: a machine's recorded operator or a nearby witness can receive credit
without proving an entire working installation.

Some entries offer fixed supplies for further use or expansion. Collect them from
the completed entry; completion does not automatically collect rewards. Leave
inventory space when collecting. Rewards are supplementary and never required
to unlock a recipe or chapter.

Journal notices announce first objective progress and completion. Opening the
journal and collecting rewards remain manual. Unlocking an entry does not show
a notice. Completion replaces a pending start notice.

The Settings bookmark opens display and notification preferences. Item images
and condition symbols can be switched independently; requirements stay readable
with both off. Notifications can be disabled or limited to completion. Changes
apply immediately and save on this client. Back/Escape returns to the same entry
and section. The Rewards tab previews rewards before completion; selecting a tab
or visiting settings does not collect anything.

Progress is individual and saved by Questlog. Entries do not consume items or
unlock recipes. Equipment conditions establish possession; suggested builds in
their descriptions still need assembly, power and testing. The glider recipe and
repair materials are unchanged: obtain phantom membranes through normal play.

Objective rows use short labels. Read the main description for full item names,
counts and conditions. Descriptions and objective rows wrap and scroll; compact mode does not restore
the old truncated rows.
